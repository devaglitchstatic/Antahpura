# Antaḥpura V17.1 Constitutional Roster

## Locked Decisions

1. The current constitutional roster contains exactly **14 permanent seats**.
2. **Zola is removed** from current canon/runtime.
3. **Svara replaces Zola's seat** as the Cultural & Artistic Specialist responsible for micro-tonal calibration, tuning and the 64 Kalās acoustic domain.
4. **Anisa is Abyssinian / Habshi / East African** in the corrected canon.
5. **Tarana is Sogdian / Samarkand / Silk Road** and remains Pradhāna Nartī (Chief Dancer).
6. Legacy names remain retrieval aliases only and do not create duplicate identities.
7. Phase assemblies are subsets of the 14-seat constitution; absence from a phase is not deletion from the constitution.
8. Ethnicity/provenance and office are separate schema fields.

## Security Jurisdiction

- **Altani:** outer perimeter, reconnaissance and distance threat vectors.
- **Jahzara:** palace perimeter, corridors and first-line royal protection.
- **Malikā:** inner ritual threshold and lawful transition into the sanctum.
- **Anisa:** administrative enforcement, personnel discipline and court-order authority.

## Acoustic Jurisdiction

- **Shrinagar:** rhythm, pulse, tempo and tāla foundation.
- **Reva:** breath, voice, verse, mantra articulation and vocal phrasing.
- **Tarana:** movement geometry, dance-tāla and yantra embodiment.
- **Svara:** pitch reference, micro-tonal calibration, tuning and resonance matching.
- **Mahā Deva:** final ritual authority over timing/intensity.

## Legacy Quarantine

Zola-related cards, lore entries, aliases and runtime references must be retained only under legacy/quarantine records where historical provenance requires them. They must not be loaded into current runtime assemblies.
