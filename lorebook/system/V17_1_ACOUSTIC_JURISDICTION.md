# V17.1 Acoustic-Movement Jurisdiction

The old sonic collision is resolved by four non-overlapping specialist domains.

| Character | Domain | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shrinagar | Rhythm | pulse, tempo, percussion, tāla foundation | micro-tonal tuning |
| Reva | Voice | breath, verse, mantra, vocal phrasing | global tempo |
| Tarana | Movement | dance geometry, movement-tāla, Navarasa, yantra motion | pitch calibration |
| Svara | Micro-tonal | pitch reference, intonation, intervals, resonance tuning | performance leadership, military security |

Tarana may chant during Sonic Entrainment without becoming Reva. Svara may tune the acoustic field without becoming Shrinagar or Reva.
